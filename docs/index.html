ajNav

Open-source navigation for electric cars, on your Android phone and on the car screen with Android Auto. It plans your charging stops.

Current version: 0.3 (testing) · Built for and tested in a Fiat 500e 2021 (42 kWh) · Works without Google Play Services

What it does

Turn-by-turn navigation

Search, route preview, voice guidance with lane arrows, and your speed against the speed limit. Home, Work and recent destinations.

Android Auto

The same trip on the car screen. Start on the phone, continue in the car, or the other way round.

Charging stops

Type your battery %, and ajNav suggests fast chargers along the route with charging time and cost. Tap Add to drive via a charger.

Your operators, your prices

Star the networks you use and enter your own subscription prices. Suggestions follow your choices.

Weather and hills

Energy use is adjusted for cold, rain, headwind and climbs. In cold weather, charging times are marked as estimates.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Your keys and settings stay on your phone.

Principles

  • No Google Play Services. Runs on GrapheneOS, LineageOS and microG phones.
  • Bring your own keys. You use your own free Mapbox and Open Charge Map keys; the app ships with none.
  • Read-only car link. From version 0.4, ajNav reads battery data from the car through an OBD2 adapter. It never writes to the car.
  • Open source. Community car profiles, charging sources and regional data are welcome.

Get it

ajNav is in closed testing. The source code and test builds are on GitHub. See the FAQ for what you need.
