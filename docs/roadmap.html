Roadmap & status

GPS navigation first, car data later. Each version is tested on real drives before the next one starts. Updated November 2026.

VersionWhat's in itStatus
0.1Phone navigation: search, route preview, voice turn-by-turn with lane guidance, speed and speed limit, Home/Work and recent destinations.Done
0.2Android Auto: map and turn-by-turn on the car screen, search, places, route preview, phone ↔ car sync.Done
0.3Charging stops with a typed battery %: chargers from Open Charge Map, your operators and prices, weather and hills, offline cache, charger list in Android Auto. Map themes (green dark, light), car logo screen, voice choices.Testing
0.4Car link: read-only Bluetooth OBD2 (OBDLink CX), live battery %, temperature and power from the Fiat 500e, a Dashboard tab. Live charger availability (NOBIL).Planned
0.5Live planning: live data feeds the planner (re-planning while driving, real battery temperature), live values in Android Auto, Swedish traffic data (Trafikverket).Planned
1.0Release after verified tests in the car.Planned
